Plan Your Trip

When to Visit

Dry seasons run from June to September and December to February—ideal conditions for gorilla trekking experiences.

Visa Requirements

Rwanda offers visas on arrival for citizens of all countries worldwide, making travel arrangements simple.

Getting Around

Safe, exceptionally clean, well-maintained roads link the capital city of Kigali easily to all major national parks.
