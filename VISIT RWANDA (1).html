DISCOVERY OF THE BEAUTY OF RWANDA

Rwandas beauty lies in its striking contrast of dramatic wilderness and peaceful, orderly communities. The western edge of the country is blessed with the stunning, deep blue waters of Lake Kivu, bordered by terraced tea plantations that paint the hillsides an emerald green. In the east, the wide-open savannahs of Akagera National Park showcase a classic African landscape where lions, rhinos, and elephants roam freely. Beyond the physical geography, Rwanda's true beauty shines through its commitment to a green, sustainable future, its exceptionally clean cities, and the joyful resilience of its culture, celebrated through traditional Intore dance and a deeply rooted spirit of unity (ubumwe).

NATURE

Unlike many typical flat savannas, Akagera features a highly scenic mix of rolling acacia hills, open grasslands, and a sprawling network of lakes and papyrus swamps. It is the largest protected wetland in Central Africa.

NYUNGWE NATIONAL PARK

It features a thrilling Canopy Walkway suspended 70 meters above the forest floor, offering stunning panoramic views of the ancient treetops, waterfalls, and rugged mountain terrain.

GORILLA PARK

It protects a significant portion of the world's remaining endangered mountain gorillas, offering world-class, highly regulated trekking experiences to encounter habituated gorilla families up close.
